# Sato Anti-Infective — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

Sato Anti-Infective is Sato Pharma's detail aid for Royafloxacin 500 mg and Saginkgo 120 mg, used by Sato Pharma's medical representatives with healthcare professionals.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account or sign-in, collects no personal or health information, and sends nothing to Sato Pharma or anyone else. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking. Everything it shows is inside the app and works without internet.

## What stays on the phone

Two settings, so the app opens the way it was left: the doctor's speciality chosen last, and the text size. They never leave the phone and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

## When something leaves the app

Only when you tap it:

- a link to the Egyptian Drug Authority's record or website opens in the phone's browser;
- the pharmacovigilance email address or hotline opens the phone's email or phone app;
- sharing a leaflet page sends that picture through the app you choose.

Those apps' own privacy policies then apply.

## Updates

The edition on Google Play is updated by Google Play. The edition downloaded from Sato Pharma's download page checks that page for a new version at most every six hours; the check sends no information about you or the phone.

## Children

The app is for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.
